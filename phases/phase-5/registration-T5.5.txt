7.  .github/workflows/ci.yml                          RESERVED — one-line risk
================================================================================
Not required, but worth a look while you are in there.

The receipt capture runs with `env -u CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR -u POTSHERD_DIR ...
HOME="$home"`. Six of the seven new paths are fixed relative to `$HOME`, but
opencode's honours `XDG_CONFIG_HOME`, so on any runner that sets it the live
receipt would name a path the committed screen does not, and the guard would
fail for a reason that has nothing to do with potsherd. Adding

  -u XDG_CONFIG_HOME

to that `env` line (both invocations) makes the capture as deterministic as the
other six paths already are. GitHub's hosted runners do not currently set it,
so this is insurance, not a fix.
