the wall-time measurement `03 §12` needs
----------------------------------------

`03 §12` asks for **ask p50 < 20 s and < $0.05 per question at k=6**. On the
subscription path, measured here, neither holds and the shortfall is not close:

    k=6, readers haiku-class at concurrency 6, synthesizer sonnet-class

    wall           57 s  ·  92 s  ·  93 s  ·  100 s  ·  183 s      (5 runs)
    of which recall  1.1 s – 12.2 s   (the vector arm; ~350 ms is the forward
                                       pass, the rest is the six-list fan-out
                                       over 236 sessions)
    per reader     18 s – 85 s, six in parallel
    spend        $0.061 – $0.123 est. for the readers alone
                 $0.15 est. for a whole k=2 run including the synthesizer

The reason is the one `03 §12` already records for `card`: a real haiku-class
call through the agent SDK is 60–160 s, not the few seconds the target was
written against. Six of them in parallel realise ~4.9x, so the reader fan-out
alone cannot finish in 20 s. **The target was written before any agent-SDK call
had been measured. Reported as a miss rather than met by narrowing k.**

`< $0.05 per question` is also missed, and it is missed in a way that has a
consequence: **`--max-usd`'s planned default of 0.10 aborts a normal k=6 run
before the synthesizer.** That is the designed behaviour working (phase-4
ruling: abort before the synthesizer rather than print an ungrounded answer),
and it fired on a real run:

    q1-ratelimit, --max-usd default 0.10
      6 readers  ·  2 found  ·  $0.123 est.  ·  92 s  ·  exit 2
      "stopped at the cost ceiling · the readers alone spent $0.123 est.
       and the synthesizer never ran."

The default is left at 0.10 because the phase plan sets it there. The
measurement says it should be about **0.30** for k=6 on the api path, and that
on the subscription path the figure it compares against is not money at all.
For the orchestrator to decide.

a defect in `llm.ts` this exposed, not fixed here
-------------------------------------------------

`Budget.admit` is checked before a call and `Budget.record` after it, so **at
concurrency 6 all six readers pass `admit` against a spend of $0** before any
of them records anything. The ceiling is therefore enforced *between* batches,
not within one, and a k=6 run can overshoot `--max-usd` by up to one full batch
before `ask` sees it. `ask` catches the overshoot after the fan-out and aborts
before the synthesizer, which is why no run here spent past the ceiling by more
than the readers' own cost — but the race is in `llm.ts`, is not T4.1's file,
and affects `card --all` at concurrency the same way.
