what the four are:

  RECENT_K                 8. Exchanges pulled when there is no `--about` and
                           no card — the G1 default slice. A constant encoding
                           a measured trade-off, so `08` rule 3 applies to it.
  expandCitationGroups()   G6. Rewrites `[id8@24, 158]` and `[id8@24, @158]`
                           into the canonical `[id8@24, id8@158]` before the
                           citation scan.
  hasMaterial()            G1. True when the prompt would carry actual session
                           material (a card, or a slice). Checked before the
                           model call and again inside `buildPrompt`.
  stripHarnessBoilerplate() G5. Removes named harness wrapper blocks from text
                           on its way into a brief.
