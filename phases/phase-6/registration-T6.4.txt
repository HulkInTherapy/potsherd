WHY THE RENDERER IS IN CORE. `renderSuggestions` sits in
`packages/core/src/link-suggest.ts` beside `MEASURED_PRECISION` rather than in
`link.ts`, and that is deliberate rather than convenient. Phase 4 measured this
rule pass at **8 of 8 genuinely absent, 1–2 of 8 worth raising**
(`phases/phase-4/evidence-T4.2/RESULTS.md`). The disclosure of that number is
the condition on which the feature is allowed to exist, so it is not something
a caller composes — it is printed by the same function that prints the rows,
and `tests/stack.test.ts` asserts it reached the output.

`suggestLinks` imports `openThreadCandidates` from `open-threads.ts` and does
not reimplement it. It never imports `linkSessions`, and a test asserts the
`links` table is untouched after a run.
